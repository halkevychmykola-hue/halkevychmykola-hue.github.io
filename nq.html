NQ market model

The market model on which strategies are tested, and all results on NQ. Every strategy followed one path: axes → simulator with switchable mechanisms → frozen run on NQ → selection by rules. Here you can open any strategy, build a portfolio and check how the result depends on the option price. ES was not touched: it is kept as the final exam.

Start with the Model tab: a synthetic minute-level market where every mechanism (volatility clustering, leverage, pension and leveraged-ETF flows, forced sellers, the Fed event, option pricing) can be switched off. Press Run, click any day to replay it minute by minute and see where the market sits in the space of axes. All results are research, not trading results; raw market data is not published.
