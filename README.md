# Mykola Halkevych — research portfolio

Independent research projects in finance (autumn 2026):

1. **NQ market model** — multi-scale model of Nasdaq-100 futures with a strict out-of-sample protocol.
2. **Tail** — distress and credit-risk model for 48 UK listed companies, compared with option prices.
3. **Circuit-1** — event-driven protocol for commodity futures after physical supply shocks.

Live site: https://halkevychmykola-hue.github.io

Interactive pages: `nq.html` (60 strategies and a portfolio builder), `tail.html` (Blotto game over 358 defences and 48 UK companies), `circuit.html` (2022 replay).

All results are research (backtests, replays, simulations), not trading results.
