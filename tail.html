Project 02 · Tail · interactive model

Tail model explorer

Two parts of the model, rebuilt for the browser from the original C++ desktop program. First, the global game: shocks drain shared supports and push on lines of defence, and a wall breaks when the push is stronger than its strength. Second, the company layer: for 48 UK listed companies, the probability of a large share-price fall, compared with what option prices imply, and the fault tree behind it.

Model inputs collected on 7 Nov 2026; company results are the model's frozen run of 8 Nov 2026 with live market data. Several inputs are placeholders, so treat the numbers as a structured view of risk, not calibrated forecasts. This is not investment advice.
Part 1 · Colonel Blotto over the financial system

Which defences break under a shock?

Pick one or more moves by other players and set their strength. Each wall's strength is its effort (1–3) reduced by the supports it relies on: budget room, central-bank balance sheet, collateral. Rule from Colonel Blotto: push greater than strength breaks the wall, a tie goes to the defender. A broken wall passes pressure on to the walls linked to it.

Part 2 · Company layer

Distress probabilities for 48 UK companies

For each company: the model's probability that the share price touches −20%, −30%, −50% and −70% before the horizon, next to the probability implied by option prices (or by volatility when no options were available). Where the model sees much more risk than the market, puts may be mispriced. Below it, the fault tree: AND/OR logic from a shock to the forced step, with a probability, a source and a sensor on each basic event.

Suggested portfolio in the frozen run

Only names where the model's probability beats the option price after a spread penalty get a size. Quarter-Kelly, capped at 5% per name and 10% in total.
